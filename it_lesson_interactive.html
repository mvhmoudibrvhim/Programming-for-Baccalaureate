How information technology developed, stage by stage — and reshaped society along the way

From a computer that filled an entire room in the 1940s, to the phone in your pocket today — this lesson shows how each stage of IT development changed the way we communicate, work, learn, and pay.

🎯 Explain the major stages in the development of information technology and their impact on society.

📍 An ordinary day for a student in Egypt

On an ordinary day, a student in Egypt checks messages on an SNS app, pays for breakfast with a cashless app, joins a lesson through online learning, and orders a book from an e-commerce (EC) shop. Twenty years ago, most of this was not possible. Information technology has developed through a series of stages — from the first computers to cloud computing — and at each stage it has changed the way people communicate, work, learn, and pay.

Moore's Law

Moore's Law is the empirical observation that the number of transistors on an integrated circuit roughly doubles about every two years, while computing power increases dramatically.

في السنين الأخيرة، الترانزستورات بقت صغيرة جدًا لدرجة إن فيه مشاكل فيزيائية زي "quantum tunneling effect" (الإلكترونات بتسرّب من الحواجز) و"leakage current" (تسريب التيار الكهربي من غير قصد)، وده بيخلي صعب تزيد الأداء وتقلل استهلاك الطاقة في نفس الوقت. عشان كده الشركات بقت تدور على طرق تانية زي المعالجات متعددة الأنوية (multi-core) والكمبيوتر الكمي (quantum computer).

🤔 Pause & think

In autonomous driving, why is it necessary to process data instantly on the vehicle side using edge computing, rather than sending the data to the cloud for judgment? Explain your answer.

Example answer

Sending camera data to the cloud and waiting for a reply takes time — even a fraction of a second. If a pedestrian steps into the road, the car needs to react in milliseconds, so it cannot afford the round trip to a distant server. Edge computing processes the data on the vehicle itself, cutting out that travel time, so the car can brake or steer in time.

Move gradually: keep both cash and cashless options available, but encourage cashless for speed and record-keeping. Reason 1 — it speeds up transactions for most customers and shop owners. Lesson Question — Answered

The guiding question asked how information technology developed and how each stage changed society. Information technology advanced through a series of stages rather than in one single leap, and each stage changed much more than just the technology itself. As computing spread and became global and mobile, everyday activities moved online, and information technology grew into a service that supports large-scale data analysis and AI. Each stage also brought social changes in how people communicate, work, learn, and make payments. Overall, the development of information technology has reshaped daily life, industry, and the way society handles information — at every single stage.

Exam-style question [6 marks]
Analyze how the spread of cloud computing (from the 2010s onward) has changed the way information technology is used. In your answer, refer to: large-scale data analysis, AI, and "IT as a service."
Model answer
Before cloud computing, any company or individual needed to buy and maintain powerful servers themselves to store or process large amounts of data. With the spread of cloud computing from the 2010s onward, it became possible to rent processing power and storage "as a service" instead of owning it — paying only for what you actually use.

This made large-scale data analysis far more accessible, since even a small company can now reach huge processing power without investing heavily in its own hardware.

Similarly, training AI models requires enormous computing power and data storage — cloud computing provides this infrastructure flexibly and on demand, which greatly accelerated progress in AI.

In short: cloud computing shifted information technology from something you had to own to something you could simply use whenever you needed it — opening the door to bigger data analysis and more powerful AI applications for everyone.
